# Advanced Tier Mental Math Drills

_(Sanity-Check Any Business Model in <10 Seconds)_

---

## Drill 1 — The “Instant Feasibility Scan”

**Purpose:** Detect if a business model is viable before details.

Ask yourself 3 questions immediately:

1. Revenue potential?
2. Cost scale?
3. Margin direction?

Example:

- Price = $20/mo
- Market size = 10k users
    

Brain shortcut:
> 20 × 10k = 200k monthly max revenue

If infra cost already $150k → weak model.

If infra cost $5k → strong model.

No spreadsheet needed.

---

## Drill 2 — Revenue Ceiling Reflex

**Purpose:** Know maximum possible revenue instantly.

Formula:

> Max revenue = price × total possible customers

Practice examples:

- $50 product × 1,000 users → 50k
    
- $100 × 10k users → 1M
    
- $30 × 100k users → 3M
    

Advanced founders instantly test:

> Is that ceiling even worth building?

---

## Drill 3 — Unit Economics Lightning Check

**Purpose:** Detect profit viability instantly.

Memorize rule:

> LTV should be ≥ 3× CAC

Example:

- CAC = $40
    
- price = $20/mo
    
- retention = 3 months
    

Revenue = 60 → LTV = 60

Compare:

- CAC = 40
    
- LTV = 60
    

→ weak business

This calculation should take <5 seconds mentally.

---

## Drill 4 — Scale Stress Test

**Purpose:** See if system breaks at scale.

Given:

- 1 server handles 20 users
    
- users projected = 1000
    

Mental math:

> 1000 ÷ 20 = 50 servers

Then:

> cost per server × 50

If that number exceeds revenue → model fails.

---

## Drill 5 — Investor Growth Projection Test

**Purpose:** Estimate startup trajectory instantly.

If growth is:

> 20% monthly

Memorize:

|Months|Multiplier|
|---|---|
|3|~1.7×|
|6|~3×|
|12|~9×|

So if startup has:

> 100 users today

12 months later ≈ **900**

If someone claims 100 → 50,000 in 1 year at 20% growth  
You instantly know:

> impossible

---

## Drill 6 — Runway Estimation

**Purpose:** Know survival time instantly.

Formula:

> runway ≈ cash ÷ monthly burn

Example:

- cash = 120k
    
- burn = 20k
    

→ 6 months

This should be automatic.

---

## Drill 7 — Load vs Revenue Reality Check

**Purpose:** Catch GPU/infra businesses that look profitable but aren’t.

Example:

- price = $30
    
- server cost = $720/mo
    
- server handles 20 users
    

Revenue/server:

> 30 × 20 = 600

Cost/server:

> 720

You instantly see:

> losing money per server

That’s advanced-level detection.

---

## Drill 8 — “Is This Worth It?” Filter

**Purpose:** Avoid building low-ceiling businesses.

Quick test:

> max possible revenue ÷ effort

Example:

- niche size = 2000 users
    
- price = $15
    

Max revenue:

> 2000 × 15 = 30k/month

Ask:

> Is $30k ceiling worth 2 years of work?

Top founders do this automatically.

---

## Drill 9 — Percentage Compression Trick

Used when someone throws percentages fast.

Memorize:

|%|Fraction|
|---|---|
|10%|1/10|
|20%|1/5|
|25%|1/4|
|33%|1/3|
|50%|1/2|

So:

> 25% of 800 = quarter = 200

Instant.

---

## Drill 10 — The “Smell Test”

The highest-level skill.

When you hear numbers, ask:

> does this _feel_ right?

Experienced founders sense when numbers are off by an order of magnitude.

Example:

Someone says:

> We'll make $1M with 200 users paying $30/month

Your brain instantly detects:

- 200 × 30 = 6000/mo
    
- yearly = 72k
    

So:

> claim is impossible

That detection should happen **before conscious math**.
