
## Core idea

The word **percent** means **per hundred**:

- **Per** means “for each.”
- **Cent** means “hundred.”

So **7%** means **7 for every 100**.

## Example: What is 7% of 300?

Think of 300 as three groups of 100:

$$
300 \div 100 = 3
$$

Each group of 100 contributes 7:

$$
7 \times 3 = 21
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{7\% \text{ of } 300 = 21}
$$

## Mental shortcut

When the number is a multiple of 100:

1. Divide the number by 100 to count the groups of 100.
2. Multiply that number by the percentage.

For 7% of 300:

$$
7 \times \left(300 \div 100\right) = 7 \times 3 = 21
$$
