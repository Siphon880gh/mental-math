## Key numbers

You recently purchased small popcorn bags from Costco so you know how much each bag costs. At a a vending machine at work, you notice one bag is being sold for $2.

- **Cost:** $0.33 per bag
- **Selling price:** $2.00 per bag
- **Dollar markup:** $1.67 per bag
- **Markup percentage, based on cost:** about **506%**
- **Price multiple:** about **6.06× cost**
- **Gross margin, based on sale price:** **83.5%**

## Mental shortcut

Divide the selling price by the cost:

$$
2 \div 0.33 \approx 6
$$

So, the product sells for about **6× its cost**.

Subtract the original **1× cost** from the **6× selling price**:

- About **5× extra** above cost
- Roughly a **500% markup**

## Formula

$$
\text{Markup percentage} = \frac{\text{Selling price} - \text{Cost}}{\text{Cost}} \times 100
$$

For this example:

$$
\frac{2.00 - 0.33}{0.33} \times 100
= \frac{1.67}{0.33} \times 100
\approx 506\%
$$

## Precision note

If the cost had been exactly **$0.3333\ldots**—or **33⅓¢**—then $2.00 would be exactly **6× cost**, which means a **500% markup**.
