
In this example, TTM is used in the context of researching failed business ideas:

TTM means Trailing Twelve Months

An AI real estate company creation platform was able to make $34k in 12 months. However they had to exit and recoup cost. They’re asking a lot more. At $34k in 12 months, that means they make less than $3k a month

Unfortunately acquire.com doesn't give you the name of the company. Because if they had, you would've been able to look them up on Crunchbase to find out their company stats such as company size (number of employees).
