# Square identities

Three identities do more work than a dozen separate tricks. Learn them as shapes:

- (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
- (a − b)² = a² − 2ab + b²
- (a + b)(a − b) = a² − b²

The third one has its own guide (Difference of squares). This guide is the first two, plus the digit patterns they produce.

## Square by splitting: (a + b)²

43² = (40 + 3)²

- 40² = 1,600
- 2 × 40 × 3 = 240
- 3² = 9
- **1,849**

## Square by rounding up: (a − b)²

98² = (100 − 2)²

- 100² = 10,000
- 2 × 100 × 2 = 400, subtract it
- 2² = 4, add it back
- **9,604**

Square near 100 is this identity with a = 100: 97² = 10,000 − 600 + 9 = **9,409**, and 104² = 10,000 + 800 + 16 = **10,816**.

## Ends in 5

n5²: n × (n+1), then glue 25. 75² → 7 × 8 = 56 → **5,625**. 125² → 12 × 13 = 156 → **15,625**. It is (10n + 5)² = 100·n(n+1) + 25.

## Same tens digit, ones add to 10

43 × 47: tens × (tens + 1) = 4 × 5 = 20, ones product 3 × 7 = 21 → **2,021**.

62 × 68 → 6 × 7 = 42, 2 × 8 = 16 → **4,216**.

## Same ones digit, tens add to 10

23 × 83: the ones match (3) and the tens add to 10 (2 + 8).

- Left: tens product plus the ones digit: 2 × 8 + 3 = **19**
- Right: ones squared, two digits: 3² = **09**
- 19 | 09 → **1,909**

64 × 44: 6 × 4 + 4 = 28, 4² = 16 → **2,816**.

## Equally far from a center

47 × 53 = 50² − 3² = 2,500 − 9 = **2,491**. 96 × 104 = 100² − 4² = **9,984**. That is the difference-of-squares identity; you just name the center.

## Conversation use

A 43-foot square booth is 1,849 square feet: 1,600 + 240 + 9. A 98 × 98 grid is 9,604 cells. You never stack columns.

## Same idea, new numbers

- 31² → 900 + 60 + 1 = 961
- 49² → 2,500 − 100 + 1 = 2,401
- 37 × 77 → 3 × 7 + 7 = 28 | 49 → 2,849
- 85² → 8 × 9 = 72 | 25 → 7,225
