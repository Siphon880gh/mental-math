# Left-to-right addition

School trains adding from the right (ones, then tens). Conversation math is faster **left to right**: lock the big place first, then pour in the rest.

## Add

47 + 36

- Tens: 40 + 30 = 70
- Ones: 7 + 6 = 13
- 70 + 13 = **83**

Keep a running total instead of writing columns:

> 47… plus 30 is 77… plus 6 is 83.

## Subtract

82 − 47

- 82 − 40 = 42
- 42 − 7 = **35**

If a place would go negative, borrow in the running total (42 cannot minus 7? It can — 35). The only hard case is crossing a hundred: 203 − 48 → 203 − 50 = 153, then add back 2 → **155** (see also round-then-compensate).

## Why operators use this

A price of $1,240 plus a $380 add-on is “1,200 + 300 = 1,500, then 40 + 80 = 120, total **1,620**.” You never need the ones column first.

## Check

- 19 + 28 → 30 + 17 = 47
- 250 + 180 → 400 + 30 = 430
- 91 − 37 → 91 − 30 = 61, minus 7 = 54
