# Same distance from a center

When two factors sit equally far from a round middle, you are looking at **a² − b²**, not a grind.

(c − d)(c + d) = c² − d²

## 48 × 52

Both are 2 away from 50.

50² = 2,500  
2² = 4  
**2,496**

99 × 101 is the same idea around 100: 10,000 − 1 = **9,999**.

## Same tens digit, ones add to 10

43 × 47: tens digit 4, ones 3+7=10.

Left: 4 × 5 = 20  
Right: 3 × 7 = 21  
**2,021**

This is the difference-of-squares identity in costume: the pair is (10t+d)(10t+(10−d)).

## Near a square you already know

73 × 77 sits on 75. 75² = 5,625 (n5 glue). Gap 2.

5,625 − 4 = **5,621**

## Conversation use

A $48 seat × 52 weeks is not “about 2,500.” It is 2,500 minus 4. The identity is the smell test and the exact answer.
