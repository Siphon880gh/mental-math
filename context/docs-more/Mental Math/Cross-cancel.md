# Cancel before you multiply

Fractions get smaller **before** the product, not after. Any numerator may cancel with any denominator.

## Cancel

6/8 × 4/3

6 and 3 share a 3 → 2/8 × 4/1  
8 and 4 share a 4 → 2/2 × 1/1 → **1**

Do not compute 24/24 and reduce later if you can cancel on sight.

## Compare without decimals

Which is larger, 7/11 or 8/13?

Cross: 7×13 = **91**, 8×11 = **88**. 91 is bigger, so **7/11** is bigger.

You never need 0.636 vs 0.615.

## Conversation use

A “we’ll take 3/8 of 2/5 of the pool” is a cancel, not four multiplies. A “is seven elevenths better than eight thirteenths?” is a cross product.

## Same idea, new numbers

- 8/12 × 9/2 = 3
- 9/15 × 10/6 = 1
- 3/4 vs 2/3 → 9 vs 8, so 3/4 is larger
