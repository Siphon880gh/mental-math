# Pair the easy factors first

Multiplication is associative and commutative. The order you *write* is not the order you should compute.

Scan for a pair that makes 10, 100, or 1,000, then dump whatever is left onto that round number.

## Associate to 100

25 × 16 × 4

Do not start 25×16. Pair 25×4 = **100**, then 100×16 = **1,600**.

## Scan the whole product

8 × 37 × 125

8×125 = 1,000. Then ×37 = **37,000**.

Left-to-right 8×37 first is the slow path on purpose.

## Distribute when one factor is a split

23 × 17 = 23×10 + 23×7, but only after you decided *not* to regroup. Factoring and distributing are the same habit: rewrite before you grind.

## Conversation use

Headcount 8 × $37 × 125 seats is “eight times an eighth of a thousand, times 37,” not three ugly multiplies.

## Same idea, new numbers

- 25 × 48 = 25×4×12 = 100×12 = 1,200
- 5 × 17 × 2 = 10×17 = 170
- 125 × 16 × 8 = 1,000×16 = 16,000
