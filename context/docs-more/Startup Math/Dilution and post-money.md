# Dilution and post-money

A priced round:

> post-money = pre-money + cash in  
> percent sold ≈ cash / post-money

## Example

Pre-money $8M, raise $2M → post-money **$10M**. You sold 2/10 = **20%**.

Founders who owned 100% now own 80% (if no option-pool shuffle).

## Option pool from pre-money

If the pool is created **before** the round, founders dilute twice (pool, then investor). Mental check: a 10% pool carved from pre-money on the example is 10% of $8M = $800k of extra paper, which is not the same as 10% of post.

You do not need the full cap table in your head. You need: **cash ÷ post-money ≈ investor percent.**

## SAFE / uncapped

If there is no price yet, you cannot finish this trick. Ask for a cap or a price.

## Conversation use

“We raised 3 on 12 pre” → post 15, sold **20%**. “We raised 3 at 12 post” is sold 3/12 = **25%**. Listen for pre vs post.

## Same idea, new numbers

- Pre 20, raise 5 → post 25, sold 20%
- Raise 1, post 5 → sold 20%
